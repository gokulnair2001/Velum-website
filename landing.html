6 Behavioral Signals
Critical
🔥

Retry Storm

Users frantically clicking the same button multiple times—a sign of latency or failed feedback.

Critical
😶

Silent Abandonment

Users leave without any interaction after viewing—no clicks, no scrolls, just gone.

Warning
🔄

Confusion Loop

Repeated back-and-forth navigation indicating uncertainty or poor information architecture.

Warning
⏸️

Hesitation

Unusual pauses before critical actions reveal anxiety, doubt, or hidden friction.

Signal
🚪

Early Dropoff

Users abandoning flows earlier than baseline—something changed or broke.

Signal
⏭️

Users skipping expected steps—either smart shortcuts or broken flows.
